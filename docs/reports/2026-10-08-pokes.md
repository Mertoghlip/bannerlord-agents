# Why the orcs were poked 125 times (October 8 session)

**In short:**
- The 125 pokes in 199 in-game hours carried 251 reasons between them, since everything in the same hour is merged into one poke. Battles were in 87 pokes, and enemy sightings in 53.
- 100 of the 122 battles were small fights: orc lords chasing bandits, and caravans or villagers being robbed. None was a siege.
- Dropping the small fights, and sightings of enemy lords far from orc land, would leave 58 pokes, about 7 a day. Those are mostly things a ruler should hear: lords captured, villages raided, enemies near or heading in.
- At the speed you played, even 58 pokes is far more than the agent can answer one at a time. The war room also has to bundle them.

Sources: the campaign's poke records (`pokes_mistymountainorcs.jsonl`) and this launch's trace. A poke can carry several reasons, so the "pokes it was in" column adds up to more than 125.

**Why cuts alone are not enough:**
- 58 pokes is still one every 3.4 in-game hours.
- At the speed you played, about a second per in-game hour, around 17 new pokes would arrive while the agent writes one answer (53 seconds, typically).
- At normal speed, about a day every 45 seconds, it would still be about 8.
- So the war room should also wake the agent only when it is free, handing it everything since its last answer in one go. The agent already taught itself to answer only the newest poke.

## Also found while sorting

- **"Raiding" too early:** an enemy lord on the way to raid a village is described as "raiding" it, even while still 65 away. It should say "on the way to raid", as sieges already say "marching to besiege".
- **One lost lord, three lines:** the lord who loses a battle shows up as "Elendara's Party", because the game takes the lord prisoner before the battle is reported. The "party wiped out" line then reads "Elendara's Party's party". The battle, the capture and the lost party could be one line.
- **Raids announced twice:** a raid on an orc village is usually announced twice as "being raided", the second time together with the fight at the village.
